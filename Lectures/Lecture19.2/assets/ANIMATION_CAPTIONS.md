# The six animations: which family, and what happened

One line each. Numbers are the funnel: designs tried → coilable → Riks-solved → feasible.

| animation | family | what happened |
|---|---|---|
| `D10_elliptical_rings_landscape.gif` | **D10** · Elliptical top and bottom rings, with a phase offset between their major axes | Breaking the rings' circular symmetry destroys coiling: only 9 of 67 designs coiled at all, none were feasible. A later 32-point sweep found every point non-coilable, including one perturbed about a degree from circular. |
| `D6_run17_rectangle_native.gif` | **D6** · Anisotropic rectangular section, radial-short and tangential-long | The one that worked, and the baseline everything else is measured against. 6 feasible of 165; becomes "run17_rectangle", later refined to 0.7704 kPa. |
| `D1_pretwisted_negative_native.gif` | **D1** · Pre-twisted longerons, a helical twist applied to each leg | Pre-twist destroys coilability rather than helping it: 6 of 46 designs coiled, none reached the second stage. Formally inconclusive only because a licence-server outage killed 26 of the planned runs. |
| `D25_tape_spring_landscape.gif` | **D25** · Thin-walled open circular arc, a tape-spring section | The largest campaign in the study, 406 designs, and none feasible. The curvature that keeps the arc locally stable is the same curvature that sets its bending-strain floor, so it can never fold locally without buckling first. |
| `D21_tensegrity_native.gif` | **D21** · Class-1 tensegrity, struts and prestressed cables replacing the bending longeron | Posted the largest stress in the whole study and was disqualified for it. The members barely strain, so it is a pin-jointed linkage rather than a supercompressible material, and not comparable to elastic bending. |
| `restudy_floor_contact.gif` | not a family — the **re-study under contact** | Ground and disc contact were restored on 2026-08-06/08, and five earlier families were re-examined under it. Only tape-spring was actually settled (best of 28 reaches 21% compression against the 80% needed); tensegrity was stopped by the floor; Kresling, laced and chiral-shell were migrated but never tested. |

**One caution that belongs with the last row**, in the deck's own words: *"Do not read 'we
migrated it' as 'we tested it.' One design cannot settle a family, and the one design available
is usually the winner of a search run without contact, the worst point to generalise from."*
