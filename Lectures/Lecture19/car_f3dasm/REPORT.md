Built the car stopping-distance dataset and surrogate. make_data.py draws 30 Sobol speeds in [3, 82] (seed 0), draws per-driver z ~ N(1.5, 0.5^2) (seed 0), computes y = z*x + 0.1*x^2, and stores an ExperimentData with inputs x, z and output y. Store layout: CSVs in data/experiment_data/ (loads via ExperimentData.from_file(project_dir='data')). predict.py fits a degree-2 sklearn polynomial to that data and exposes predict(x). Uncertain: with 30 noisy drivers the fit deviates from the mean curve 1.5x+0.1x^2 by up to ~11.5 m, and noise grows with speed. No ground-truth evaluator was registered; 0 evals reported.
