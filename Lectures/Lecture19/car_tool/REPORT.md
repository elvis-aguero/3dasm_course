Model written to predict.py (predict(x) -> (mu, sd)). Mean mu(x) = 1.581·x + 0.0982·x² (m), i.e. an effective reaction time of ~1.58 s plus a braking term with implied deceleration ~5.1 m/s². Spread sd(x) = 0.418·x (m), the maximum-likelihood fit among linear, power-law and mu-proportional forms (power-law exponent came out 1.06, so proportional-to-speed is a sound choice). All 30 Measure calls were used (30 evals reported). Checks: in-sample 95% band covers 29/30 points (96.7%); leave-one-out coverage with the fixed sd is also 29/30 (96.7%). Replicates at x=3 (4.29, 4.02, 5.59) and x=82 (766.5, 776.6, 804.4) agree with the mean to within ~1–2%. Remaining uncertainty: only 30 noisy points, with 2–3 replicates at each end and single measurements elsewhere, so the sd shape in the 15–70 m/s mid-range is rough (per-bin estimates ~0.1–0.13 relative at low/mid speeds, ~0.04–0.07 at high speed); sd at very low x (<5) may be slightly overstated.
